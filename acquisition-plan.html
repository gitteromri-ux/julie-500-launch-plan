Calculation assumptions: 90% of clicks load the LP; per 100 LP visits, 4 add to cart, 2 initiate checkout and 1.5 purchase. Acquisition costs: $160 → $70 → $55 → $34 → $29. CPM ceiling: $20. Figures are rounded for display; calculations retain full precision. These are planning assumptions, not verified campaign results.
