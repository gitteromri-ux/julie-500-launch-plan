What we learned from the masterclass

Creative

Ad#17, a 69-second customer video of a woman, made 70 of 122 purchases (57%) at $310 each; campaign average $383. Static images sold almost nothing.

Placement

Facebook Reels: 101 of 122 purchases (83%) at $9.96 a landing page view. Instagram feed: $1,885 for 2.

Audience

Ages 55+ made 104 of 122 purchases (85%). Ages 35–44 bought once.

Cheap clicks

Optimising for add to cart gave 364 adds to cart and 0 purchases for $11,381. Cheap Facebook feed and Threads views did not buy. Every campaign here optimises for purchase.

Context only: Blueprint's earlier $179-a-month offer and ads sold 3 times for $16,544; this plan uses a new $79 entry and the masterclass's winning ads, so that history is not used as the predictor.

Safeguards

Gates

Gate 0, before any spend: page shows the new prices, one test purchase shows in Meta and the CRM. Day 5: the minimum purchases and adds to cart in each week 1 check, or stop. Gate B, end of week 2: cost per purchase within 1.5 times target, or cold budget drops to $150 a day.

Kill rules

An ad spending $300 (Blueprint $500) with 0 adds to cart is paused. An ad set spending 2 times target with 0 checkouts is paused. Ceilings: cost per landing page view above $16 for 3 days; frequency above 3 cold or 5 warm in 7 days.

Caps and stop-loss

Daily budgets $700, so a week is $4,900, under the $5,000 cap. Budget changes at most 20%, once per 48 hours. A week above 2.5 times target cost per purchase: next week warm audiences only; two such weeks: all ads stop.

Who decides

Omri, at the weekly go / no-go meeting. Gita proposes each pause or change; nothing changes in Meta without Omri's go. Julie's ad sets stay frozen until 26 October.
