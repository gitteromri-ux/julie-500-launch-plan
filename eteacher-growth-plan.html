eTeacher growth plan

A 4-week media plan for Harel, CEO of eTeacher: Blueprint and French Atelier (Culture Capsules first). Built on what the masterclass ads actually did, with gates, kill rules and a weekly stop-loss. Prepared by Omri Gitter, updated 19 October 2026, Israel time.

Summary · cap $10,000 a week

A media plan that spends only as fast as the evidence allows: warm audiences first, cold traffic only after a gate is passed.

What we learned from the masterclass

CREATIVE

One video sold: Ad#17, a 69-second customer video of a woman, made 70 of 122 Meta purchases (57%) at $310 each, against $383 for the whole campaign. Static images sold almost nothing.

PLACEMENT

Facebook Reels took $36,131 of $46,723 and 101 of 122 purchases (83%). Instagram feed: $1,885 for 2 purchases.

AGE

Buyers are 55+: 104 of 122 purchases (85%). Ages 35–44 bought once.

CHEAP CLICKS

Cheap traffic does not buy. Blueprint's Facebook feed views at $2.28 gave 4,535 views and 2 purchases; Threads views at $0.20 gave none. Optimising for add to cart gave 364 adds to cart, 18 checkouts and 0 purchases for $11,381.

PRICE

At $179 the first month, Blueprint sold 3 times for $16,544 since 3 September: 6,279 views, 82 adds to cart (1.3%; Julie 4.4%), 30 checkouts, 3 purchases (10% of checkouts; Julie 69%). Warm visitors wanted it (27% added to cart) but stopped at payment.

Plain read. On the evidence, Blueprint cold traffic costs about $1,149 a sale ($10.68 per view at Julie's cost, converting at a third of the masterclass), so its prospecting stays small unless Gate B proves otherwise. Sequence and gates

NOW–26 OCT

Julie's ad sets stay frozen. Gate 0 work only: page prices, a test purchase, audiences. No new spend.

28 OCT

Julie's live event ends 27 October, 7 PM Eastern (2 AM Israel time on 28 October). Blueprint opens to the people who just watched it, by email first, then a warm ad set. Capsules opens the same week if its Gate 0 is passed.

GATE A

Campaign structure

CAMPAIGN

One sales campaign optimised for purchase. Not add to cart: that gave 364 adds to cart and 0 purchases. Fewer than 50 purchases a week means Meta's learning stays limited, so there are only 2 ad sets and few edits.

B1 WARM

$150 a day from 28 October. Masterclass buyers (121 in the CRM on 19 October), live-event attendees, registrants, Julie and Blueprint adds to cart and checkouts in the last 30 days, site visitors in the last 30 days (1,300–1,600). Excludes Blueprint buyers. Ads: Julie's bridge video "your next step after the masterclass", $79 to start, a glucose monitor in the box; VIP for buyers.

B2 COLD

$150 a day in week 1. US, ages 45–65, Facebook Reels first, Julie's interest stack plus a 1% lookalike of masterclass buyers; excludes all warm audiences. Ads: an Ad#17-style 60–75 second customer video for Blueprint, a woman aged 55+, plus Ad#17 itself pointed at Blueprint.

PAGE

Before any spend: $79 + 4 × $169, VIP $99 + 4 × $199 with its 4 extras, a real start date, 14-day refund, Lingo glucose monitor shown, mobile checkout tested with one real purchase. Needs Omri's go.

EMAIL, ORGANIC

The offer at the end of the live event; 3 emails to attendees and buyers in week 1, then 1 a week; Julie's social posts. Counted as 12.5% organic only.

Gates

GATE 0

Page fixed, one test purchase visible in Meta and the CRM, audiences built. Otherwise no spend.

GATE A

End of week 1: B1 has at least 2 purchases or 6 checkouts, and warm visitors add to cart at 10% or more. Pass: B2 to $400 a day. Fail: B2 off, fix the page or checkout.

GATE B

End of week 2: B2 cost per sale at or under $750 (1.5 times $499), or cost per checkout at or under $350 with 2+ purchases. Pass: up to $700 a day. Fail: B2 back to $150 a day with new customer-video ads.

Safeguards: kill rules, caps and stop-loss

AD

An ad that spends $500 with 0 adds to cart is paused.

AD SET

An ad set that spends $998 (2 times the $499 target) with 0 checkouts is paused.

CEILINGS

Cost per landing page view above $16.00 cold or $24.00 warm for 3 days; cost per add to cart above $250; cost per checkout above $350; frequency (times one person sees the ads in 7 days) above 3 cold or 5 warm. Any of these: swap the ads or narrow the audience.

CAPS

Daily budgets add up to at most $700, so the week cannot pass $4,900 (Meta can spend up to 75% over a daily budget on one day, but keeps the week within 7 times the daily budget). Budget changes: at most 20% at a time, at most once every 48 hours.

STOP-LOSS

A week above $1,250 per paid sale (2.5 times the target; Julie's own first week was 2.15 times its later average) means next week runs the warm ad set only. Two such weeks: all ads stop until the weekly meeting decides.

Visitors do not add to cartPage or offerBelow 2% cold or 10% warm: fix the page (price, proof, first-month offer). Needs Omri's go.
Carts do not payCheckout or priceCheckout → purchase below 40%: test the checkout and how the payment plan is shown. This is where Blueprint failed at $179: 30 checkouts, 3 purchases.
Meta and CRM disagreeTrackingSales in the CRM but not in Meta (or the reverse): stop adding budget until the purchase event is fixed.
Everything works, cost still too highScaleDo not add budget. Keep the warm ad set; the weekly meeting decides to hold or stop.

Terms

Warm, coldWarm: people who already know us (buyers, visitors, leads). Cold: new people.
Ad setA group of ads in Meta with one budget and audience.
ViewA landing page view: the click fully loaded the sales page.
LookalikeNew people Meta finds who resemble a list we give it.
Optimised for purchaseMeta shows ads to people likely to buy, not just click.
LearningA new ad set's first days, when Meta is still finding buyers. It needs about 50 purchases a week to finish (Meta Business Help Centre).
UGCUser-generated content: a video of a real customer talking.
FrequencyAverage times one person saw the ads.
Paid, blendedPaid: ad spend ÷ ad sales. Blended: ad spend ÷ all sales, organic included.
ESTIMATE = projection from the stated assumptions, not a result. ASSUMPTION = a value chosen where no data exists, for Omri to confirm. Updated 19 October 2026, Israel time.
