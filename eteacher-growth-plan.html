VIPThe premium Blueprint package with extra personal services.
Organic salesSales that come without an ad click: email, social posts, word of mouth.
ROASReturn on ad spend: revenue ÷ ad spend. 2.00× means $2 of revenue per $1 of ads. Revenue, not profit.
Cash collectedMoney actually received in the period: first payments plus any installments already due.
Full contract valueEverything a buyer agrees to pay over all installments.
Landing page viewA visitor whose click fully loaded the sales page.
Add to cart, checkoutThe visitor chose the course, then opened the payment page.
