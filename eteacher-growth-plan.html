Meta learning: the purchase signal is thin

Meta (the company behind Facebook and Instagram ads) says an ad set leaves its "learning phase" after about 50 results in the week after its last significant edit. Blueprint gets 10–13 purchases a week, so optimizing for purchases it will stay "learning limited": less stable and dearer. Each week also brings about 33–43 adds to cart, 30–39 checkouts and 880–1,140 landing page views, so the meeting judges those first. Options for Omri: one ad set per effort so nothing splits the signal, few edits, or a test optimizing for checkouts.

Terms

Learning phaseThe first days of a new ad set, while Meta works out who buys. Cost per sale is higher and jumps around.
Ad setA group of ads in Meta sharing one budget and audience.
ImpressionsTimes an ad was shown.
Realistic vs ceiling ROASRealistic counts only payments we expect to arrive; ceiling assumes every payment arrives.
Pays backThe month when cash collected from these 4 weeks of sales passes the 4 weeks of ad spend.
Cost per saleAd spend ÷ sales. Paid only = spend ÷ sales from ads. Blended = spend ÷ all sales, including organic.
VIPThe premium Blueprint package with extra personal services.
Organic salesSales that come without an ad click: email, social posts, word of mouth.
ROASReturn on ad spend: revenue ÷ ad spend. 2.00× means $2 of revenue per $1 of ads. Revenue, not profit.
Cash collectedMoney actually received in the period: first payments plus any installments already due.
Full contract valueEverything a buyer agrees to pay over all installments.
Landing page viewA visitor whose click fully loaded the sales page.
Add to cart, checkoutThe visitor chose the course, then opened the payment page.
Meta, CRMMeta runs Facebook and Instagram ads. CRM is eTeacher's customer and order system.
GO / FIX / STOPThe weekly decision: keep the budget, keep it but change creative or page, or pause.
ESTIMATE = projection from the stated assumptions, not a result. ASSUMPTION = a value chosen where none was given, for Omri to confirm. Updated 19 October 2026, Israel time.
