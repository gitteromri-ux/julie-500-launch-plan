Terms

Cost per saleAd spend ÷ sales. Paid only counts sales from ads; blended counts all sales, including organic.
Organic salesSales without an ad click: email, social posts, word of mouth.
Landing page viewA visitor whose click fully loaded the sales page.
Add to cart, checkoutThe visitor chose the product, then opened the payment page.
Learning phaseThe first days of a new ad set, while Meta works out who buys; cost per sale is higher and unstable.
Ad setA group of ads in Meta with one budget and audience.
ImpressionsTimes an ad was shown.
Meta, CRMMeta runs Facebook and Instagram ads. CRM is the customer and order system.
VIPThe premium Blueprint package with personal extras.
GO / FIX / STOPThe weekly decision: keep the budget; keep it but change ads or page; or pause.
ESTIMATE = projection from the stated assumptions, not a result. ASSUMPTION = a value chosen where none was given, for Omri to confirm. Updated 19 October 2026, Israel time.
